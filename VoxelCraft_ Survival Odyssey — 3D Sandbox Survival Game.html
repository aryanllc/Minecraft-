Controls & Survival Guide
Desktop Mouse/Keyboard & Mobile Multi-Touch
🖥️ Desktop Controls
  • WASD: Move / Strafe
  • Space: Jump / Swim Up / Fly Up
  • Shift / Ctrl: Sprint / Crouch
  • Left-Click: Mine Block / Attack / Shoot Bow
  • Right-Click: Place Block / Use Station / Eat Food
  • 1–9 or Scroll: Select Hotbar Slot

📱 Mobile Touch & Survival Tips
  • Bottom-Left Joystick: Smooth 360° movement
  • Right-Side Swipe: Look & aim crosshair
  • Action Cluster: Break, Place/Use, Jump, Sprint, Sneak, Fly/Cam
